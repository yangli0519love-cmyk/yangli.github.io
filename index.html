01 — About

I work at the intersection of multimodal intelligence and robotics.

My research asks a simple question: how can a robot continuously understand a complex scene, use prior knowledge, learn from interaction, and turn multimodal reasoning into reliable physical actions?

I have experience across the full robotics pipeline — from algorithm design and multimodal perception to simulation, system integration, manipulation control and real-robot deployment. I am particularly interested in general-purpose agent architectures that unify perception, memory, planning and action for long-horizon open-world tasks.

03 — Selected Work

Publications & research projects

2027
Open-World ManipulationActive PerceptionMemory

Think Before You Act: Knowledge-Driven Continuous Manipulation for Open-World Robotic Tasks

Introduces REAM, a knowledge-driven active manipulation framework that combines commonsense and episodic memory with real-time visual feedback to guide target search, interaction and dynamic replanning under occlusion, containment and out-of-view conditions.

ICRA 2027 submission · REAM / SCOPE
2026
Zero-ShotRobot AgentSkill Graph

UniManip: General-Purpose Zero-Shot Robotic Manipulation with Agentic Operational Graph

Builds a general-purpose zero-shot manipulation system around an Agentic Operational Graph, enabling multimodal task decomposition, reusable skill composition, parameter adaptation and closed-loop execution across objects, spatial perturbations and long-horizon tasks.

2026
Dexterous ManipulationAffordanceVLM

AnyDexManip: Multi-Fingered Dexterous Manipulation with VLM-Guided Affordance Reasoning and Spatial Understanding

Combines VLM-guided affordance reasoning with spatial understanding to infer interaction regions and task constraints for multi-fingered dexterous manipulation, targeting stronger zero-shot generalization to unseen objects and diverse tasks.

2026
Object NavigationVision-LanguageExploration

VLAI: Exploration and Exploitation Based on Visual-Language Aligned Information for Robotic Object Goal Navigation

Studies visual-language aligned cues for balancing exploration and exploitation in object-goal navigation, using semantic priors to reduce inefficient search and improve target-directed navigation in unknown environments.

2026
LLM RobustnessChinese NLPHuman Input Noise

Beyond Phonetic Similarity: Unraveling LLM Robustness under Realistic Keyboard Mis-typing Perturbations

Investigates LLM robustness under realistic Chinese keyboard mistyping, modeling perturbations from keyboard geometry and user input behavior and evaluating their impact across multiple tasks and language models.
