02 / ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Hands-on by choice.

I’m interested in a simple question: how can a robot make sense of a complex world, and decide what to do next?

My work spans active perception, knowledge- and memory-guided planning, multimodal agents, and robotic manipulation. I’m particularly drawn to tasks where a robot needs to look again, interact, or revise its assumptions before it can act effectively.

I enjoy connecting algorithm design with working systems: building perception–planning–control pipelines, evaluating them in simulation, and debugging their behavior on real robots. My longer-term interest is in more general robot agents for open-world interaction, manipulation, and control.
